VoyageBuddy: Official Guide

Welcome to VoyageBuddy! This in-depth guide will teach you how to master the platform, stay safe, and find the perfect travel companions across Namibia and beyond.

1. Setting Up Your Profile

Your profile is your travel resume. A complete profile dramatically increases your chances of finding a great buddy.

  • Category: Choose whether you Have Means (a car/budget), Need Means (looking for a ride), or Have Both (flexible).
  • Verification: Uploading your ID gives you a verified badge (the blue shield). Verified users get 80% more requests.
  • Vibe & Interests: Be honest! If you like quiet road trips, say so. If you love loud music, put it in your bio.

2. Finding Travel Buddies

Head to the Search tab to browse the community.

  • Use the Filters icon to narrow down users by Town, Gender, Trip Type, and Verification status.
  • Check a user's Star Rating. This rating is an average of reviews left by people who have traveled with them before.
  • Click Message to introduce yourself before requesting to join their trip.

3. The Trip Board

The Trip Board is the core of VoyageBuddy. It shows all upcoming journeys.

  • Posting a Trip: Click "Post Trip". You can now enter your Origin, intermediate Stops, and Destination. This allows users along your route to hop in!
  • Cost Splitting: When posting, select how costs are split (e.g., Equal, Driver Pays Fuel, Percentage). This prevents awkward conversations later.
  • Requesting to Join: See a trip you like? Click "Join". The trip owner will be notified and can Accept or Decline your request.

4. Notifications & Chat

VoyageBuddy keeps you connected in real-time.

  • Notification Bell: Located on the Home screen. You will be alerted when someone likes your post, comments, or requests to join your trip.
  • Inbox: Access your chats via the bottom navigation bar. Use the chat to coordinate pickup times and share playlists!

5. Trust & Safety Guidelines

Your safety is our top priority.

  • Always meet in public for the first pickup or departure.
  • Check Verification: Look for the blue shield icon before agreeing to a trip.
  • Share your trip: Use the "Share" button on a trip card to send your itinerary to a friend or family member outside the app.
  • SOS Button: In an absolute emergency, use the red SOS button found in the bottom right corner of the app.
